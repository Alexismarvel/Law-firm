Practice Areas

Comprehensive legal services tailored to meet your needs.

Home / Practice Areas

Corporate Law

We provide full-spectrum corporate legal services including business formation, contract drafting and negotiation, mergers and acquisitions, corporate governance, securities compliance, and partnership disputes. Our corporate clients range from startups to multinational corporations.

Family Law

Our family law practice covers divorce, legal separation, child custody and visitation, child and spousal support, property division, adoption, prenuptial agreements, and domestic violence protection orders. We approach each case with sensitivity and discretion.

Criminal Defense

From traffic violations to serious felonies, our criminal defense team provides aggressive representation. We handle DUI/DWI, drug offenses, white-collar crimes, assault, theft, and federal criminal charges. Every client is presumed innocent and deserves a vigorous defense.

Property Law

Our property law practice encompasses residential and commercial real estate transactions, title disputes, landlord-tenant litigation, zoning and land use, eminent domain, and property tax appeals. We protect your property interests with diligence and expertise.

Estate Planning

We help clients plan for the future through wills, living trusts, irrevocable trusts, powers of attorney, healthcare directives, and estate tax planning. Our goal is to ensure your assets are protected and your wishes are honored.

Civil Litigation

Our civil litigation team represents clients in contract disputes, personal injury claims, business torts, employment disputes, and civil rights cases. We are trial-ready and committed to achieving the best possible outcome through negotiation or courtroom advocacy.
