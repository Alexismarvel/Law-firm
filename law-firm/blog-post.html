Recent Changes in Family Law: What You Need to Know

Insert Featured Image

The landscape of family law continues to evolve, and 2026 has brought several significant legislative changes that affect families across the state. At Marvel & Associates, we believe it is essential for our clients to stay informed about these developments.

New Custody Guidelines

Perhaps the most impactful change comes in the area of child custody. The new legislation emphasizes the "best interest of the child" standard with greater specificity. Courts are now required to consider additional factors, including:

  • The child's own wishes, taking into account their age and maturity
  • The mental and physical health of all parties involved
  • Each parent's willingness to foster a relationship with the other parent
  • The stability of each proposed home environment

Alimony Reform

Alimony calculations have also been updated. The new formula provides clearer guidelines for determining the amount and duration of spousal support. Key changes include:

  • A cap on the duration of alimony based on the length of the marriage
  • Greater consideration of each party's earning capacity
  • Provisions for modification upon significant life changes such as retirement or remarriage

Streamlined Divorce Proceedings

For uncontested divorces, the process has been streamlined to reduce court backlog and expedite resolutions. Couples who can reach mutual agreements on all major issues may now qualify for an expedited process that significantly reduces time and legal costs.

How We Can Help

Navigating these changes can be complex. Our family law team stays current with every legislative development to provide our clients with accurate, strategic advice. Whether you are considering divorce, negotiating custody, or seeking to modify an existing order, we are here to guide you.
